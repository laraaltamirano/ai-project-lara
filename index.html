AI explained for tech dummies

At this point, if you are reading this and you haven’t heard about AI you are either not living on earth or a bit old.

But even if you haven’t, that’s okay. No judgement. This is a guide for understanding how Artificial Intelligence came to be, how it works, its advantages and disadvantages, and other facts you should know; explained for tech dummies (by a tech dummy).

The start of AI

  1. Birth of AI: The first time AI was formally established was during the Dartmouth Workshop, a summer convention held in 1956 that united researchers, mathematicians, engineers and scientists to discuss machine learning. Here, the computer scientist John McCarthy coined the term "Artificial Intelligence.”
  2. AI Winters: Known as the period where people lost interest in AI. It happened throughout the 60s up to the 90s. The severely limited computer hardware and the complex task of emulating intelligence made the progress very slow, and eventually stopped. Therefore, the government and other entities cut funding.
  3. Machine learning & deep learning: Researchers moved from trying to write individual coding rules to trying to teach computers to read patterns by feeding them huge amounts of data. This triggered deep learning in 2012: using many layers of artificial neural networks that mimic human brain cells. This is the start of machines recognizing images, processing speech, language translation, etc.
  4. Gen AI era (present time): The start of the “transformer architecture” allowed for machines to process vast amounts of text simultaneously. This paved the way for Chat GPT, Claude and other similar AI tools that today can generate images, videos, hold human-like conversations, and many other features.

Source: History of Artificial Intelligence, Wikipedia
