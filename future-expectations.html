Future expectations

There is a lot of debate regarding the (not so far away) future of AI. The main concerns are environmental footprint, safety concerns regarding ethics and its regulation and the possible development of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI, an artificial intelligence that surpasses human capacities).

Note: These are the latest news. AI develops fast, so this list could change in months or even weeks.

These is what the main news outlets are reporting lately:

Concerns about the environment, pollution and usage of natural resources:

  • Scottish data centres powering AI already using enough water to fill 27 million bottles a year - BBC
  • Data Centers and Your Power Bill - The New York Times
